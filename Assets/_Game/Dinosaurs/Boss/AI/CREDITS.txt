Face tracking model
------------------
face_landmark.onnx is the MediaPipe Face Mesh landmark model (face_landmark.tflite from MediaPipe 0.10.14),
converted to ONNX with tf2onnx. The face mesh triangles in Scripts/FaceMeshTopology.cs come from MediaPipe's
canonical_face_model.obj.

MediaPipe, Copyright Google LLC, Apache License 2.0: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The model runs locally on the CPU through Unity's Inference Engine (com.unity.ai.inference). Pictures are not
sent anywhere.

Boss face photos
================

Put the photo for the boss's face here (.jpg or .png). It is imported ready to use automatically.
This folder is kept out of git (see .gitignore here), so the photo stays on this computer and is
not pushed to GitHub. Only use a photo of someone who has agreed to it.

Taking the photo
- Straight on, looking at the camera, neutral face, even light (no strong shadow on one side).
- Plain background if possible.
- The whole head in frame: chin to the top of the hair, ear to ear.

Using it
1. Drop the photo into this folder.
2. Select the Boss in the scene (DinoTest), find the Boss Face component, and drag the photo into "Preset Photo".
3. Adjust "Photo Crop" until the head fills the picture: the left/right edges at the ears, the bottom at the chin,
   the top at the top of the hair, and the eyes about 55% of the way up. The Scene view updates as you change it.
4. Tick "Mirror" if the photo is a mirrored selfie.

A live scan from the FaceScanStation (if one is used) takes priority over this photo.
